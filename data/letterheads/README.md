# Fund letterheads

The public repository ships **placeholder stationery only**. `fund_1_source.pdf` is
generated by `scripts/make_sample_stationery.py` and has the same geometry as a
real A4 letterhead:

- A4 body with a 3.75 cm top margin and a 2.75 cm bottom margin
- Logo block, black bar, red bar, address and footer in the master PDF
- Other funds keep the same layout and only change the fund name/address

For real use, replace `fund_1_source.pdf` with your own stationery PDF, delete the
`*_header.png` / `*_footer.png` strips and restart the app to regenerate them.

Operators can drag, select, and drop the header and footer strips on the page.
